About IMMERSE

IMMERSE (Immersive Media Makers Exploring Reality, Simulation, and Experiences) is a student-led organization affiliated with the Immersive Media Design department at the University of Maryland. The club serves as a multidisciplinary hub for students to engage with immersive media through social, academic, and career-driven programs, including skill-based workshops, research panels, industry collaborations, mentorship programs, and design challenges.

IMMERSE supports creative exploration, academic growth, and professional development in immersive technologies while fostering a strong, inclusive community at the intersection of art and technology.

We're proud to be endorsed by the Immersive Media Design department at UMD.
